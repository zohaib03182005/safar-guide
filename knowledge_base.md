# Northern Areas Travel Guide — Knowledge Base
Figures are approximate (2025–2026 estimates). The voice agent must present
prices as estimates and advise travelers to confirm current rates.

## Destinations

### Hunza Valley (Gilgit-Baltistan)
- Main towns: Karimabad, Aliabad, Gulmit (Gojal), Sost.
- Top spots: Baltit Fort, Altit Fort, Attabad Lake, Hussaini Suspension Bridge,
  Passu Cones, Khunjerab Pass (Pak-China border, 4,693 m).
- Best season: May–October. Cherry blossom: late March–April. Autumn colors: October.
- Road: Karakoram Highway (KKH) from Islamabad via Mansehra–Besham or via
  Hazara Motorway → Mansehra → KKH. Islamabad → Karimabad ≈ 780–820 km,
  14–18 hours driving with stops.
- Budget hint: 4–5 day Hunza trip for 2 people ≈ Rs 80,000–120,000
  (mid-range hotels, own car), excluding shopping.

### Skardu (Gilgit-Baltistan)
- Top spots: Shangrila Resort (Lower Kachura Lake), Upper Kachura Lake,
  Shigar Fort, Khaplu, Deosai Plains (3,500–4,000 m, "Land of Giants"),
  Satpara Lake.
- Best season: June–September. Deosai accessible roughly June–September only.
- Road: Islamabad → Skardu via KKH → Jaglot → Skardu road ≈ 630–660 km,
  12–15 hours. Skardu–Jaglot road is now mostly carpeted.
- Flights: PIA flies Islamabad–Skardu (weather-dependent, often cancelled).

### Naran & Kaghan Valley
- Top spots: Saif-ul-Malook Lake, Lulusar Lake, Babusar Top (4,173 m),
  Shogran, Siri Paye.
- Season: Naran town opens ~mid-May; Babusar Top opens ~mid-June, closes
  ~October/November with snow.
- Road: Islamabad → Naran ≈ 240–260 km, 6–8 hours via Mansehra–Balakot.
- Note: Saif-ul-Malook jeep track — hire local jeeps (~Rs 4,000–6,000 round trip).

### Swat Valley
- Top spots: Malam Jabba (ski resort), Kalam, Mahodand Lake, Ushu Forest,
  Bahrain, Madyan.
- Best season: May–September for green valleys; December–February for snow/skiing.
- Road: Islamabad → Mingora ≈ 250–280 km, 5–7 hours via Swat Motorway.

### Fairy Meadows & Nanga Parbat
- Base: Raikot Bridge on KKH → jeep to Tato village → 3–4 hour trek to
  Fairy Meadows (3,300 m). Nanga Parbat view point ("killer mountain").
- Season: June–September. Trek is moderate; not for complete beginners alone.
- Jeep Raikot → Tato ≈ Rs 8,000–12,000 round trip (shared/private).

## Distances & drive times (from Islamabad, approx)
- Naran: ~250 km / 6–8 h
- Swat (Mingora): ~260 km / 5–7 h
- Hunza (Karimabad): ~800 km / 14–18 h
- Skardu: ~640 km / 12–15 h
- Khunjerab Pass (from Karimabad): ~180 km / 4–5 h one way

## Road & weather rules
- Always check weather before Babusar Top, Deosai, Khunjerab — snow can close
  passes any time outside June–September.
- KKH landslide-prone stretches: Besham–Dasu–Chilas section. Travel in daylight.
- Fuel up at Mansehra/Besham/Chilas/Gilgit — long gaps with no fuel on KKH.
- Mobile signals: SCO (Special Communications Organization) SIM works best in
  Gilgit-Baltistan; Jazz/Telenor patchy; Zong decent in towns.

## Safety & etiquette
- Carry original CNIC; multiple army/police checkposts — cooperate politely.
- Foreign tourists need NOC for some restricted zones (check latest rules).
- Respect local culture: dress modestly, ask before photographing people,
  especially women.
- Altitude: Khunjerab/Deosai/Babusar are 3,500–4,700 m — ascend gradually,
  stay hydrated, watch for headaches/nausea (AMS symptoms).
- Littering: carry trash back; many valleys now fine polluters.
- Emergency numbers: Police 15, Tourist Police helpline 1422 (verify current).

## What to pack (agent's standard advice)
- Warm layers even in summer (nights 5–10°C in Hunza/Skardu).
- Power bank, torch, basic medicines, motion-sickness tablets for KKH.
- Cash: ATMs scarce beyond Gilgit/Skardu main towns.
